01 · Taah Billa

Riverfront stay.
Local Tavira.

Taah Billa sits on Rua José Pires Padinha in Tavira’s historic centre, facing the Gilão River and close to Jardim do Coreto. The property is compact, personal and positioned for guests who want to explore the town on foot.

03 · Hosted by Pedro

Hospitality with
a local point of view.

Pedro has managed Taah Billa since 2015 and has a background in Hotel Management. His own description focuses on helping guests discover “the real Tavira,” and recent reviews repeatedly mention his communication and local help.

A direct website should make Pedro’s role and local knowledge part of the experience—not hide the property behind an OTA listing.
04 · Historic Tavira

The river is
your front door.

Restaurants, cafés and bars are within a few minutes’ walk, Tavira Castle is about four minutes away, and the boats connecting to Ilha de Tavira leave around 200 metres from the guesthouse.
